# Scholarship & Educational Grant Selection Policy

**The Education Foundation** — Draft for board adoption

This policy serves two purposes: (1) it is the source material for **Form 1023 Schedule H** (Organizations Providing Scholarships, Fellowships, Educational Loans, or Other Educational Grants), which asks the IRS applicant to describe its selection criteria, selection committee, fund supervision, and record-keeping; and (2) once adopted by the Board, it governs how the Foundation actually selects and funds students. The IRS expects the described procedures to match real practice — adopt it before the first disbursement and follow it.

## 1. Purpose of the Program

The Foundation provides need-based educational grants to underprivileged students to cover tuition, examination fees, books, school supplies, uniforms, and other direct educational expenses. Grants advance the Foundation's exempt charitable and educational purposes under IRC Section 501(c)(3). Grants are not compensation, are not loans, and confer no benefit on the Foundation, its donors, directors, officers, or their families.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

An applicant is eligible if all of the following are met:

1. **Enrollment**: currently enrolled in, or admitted to, a recognized primary school, secondary school, or accredited post-secondary institution.
2. **Financial need**: documented annual household income below [threshold — e.g., ₹3,00,000 / local poverty benchmark], or comparable evidence of hardship (loss of earning parent, displacement, disability in family).
3. **Academic standing**: passing academic standing at their current institution. [Optional: minimum percentage/GPA — keep low or omit; the program is need-based, not merit-based.]
4. **Age**: under [25] years old at the time of application.
5. **Application**: a complete application submitted through the Foundation's platform, including required documentation (Section 3).

**Categorical exclusions.** The following are ineligible regardless of need:
- Relatives (by blood, marriage, or adoption) of the Foundation's directors, officers, employees, selection-committee members, or substantial contributors.
- Any individual designated by a donor as a condition of a donation (see Section 6).

## 3. Application and Verification

Each application must include:
- Proof of enrollment or admission (school letter, ID card, or fee demand notice)
- Household income documentation (income certificate, ration card category, or employer letter; sworn declaration where documents are unavailable)
- Most recent academic records
- Fee structure or cost estimate from the institution
- Government-issued identification of the student and guardian

Foundation staff or local partner representatives verify each application before it reaches the selection committee: institution confirmation, financial-need assessment, and a home or school visit where practicable. Only verified applications ("approved" profiles on the platform) are eligible for funding.

## 4. Selection Criteria and Process

The selection committee evaluates verified applications using a documented scoring rubric weighing:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Severity of financial need | 50% |
| Educational continuity risk (likelihood of dropout without aid) | 25% |
| Academic engagement and effort (attendance, teacher recommendation) | 15% |
| Funding gap not covered by other aid or government schemes | 10% |

Selections are made [quarterly / monthly] from the pool of verified applicants, in rubric-score order, up to available funds. The committee documents the basis for each selection and each denial. No factor may include race, religion, caste, gender, or ethnicity except as a neutral tiebreaker favoring historically excluded groups is [permitted/not permitted — decide with counsel].

## 5. Selection Committee

- Composed of [3] members appointed by the Board, at least [2] of whom are independent (not officers, employees, or substantial contributors).
- No member may participate in evaluating an applicant with whom they have a family, financial, or personal relationship; such conflicts must be disclosed and recorded in the minutes.
- Donors are not committee members and have no vote on selections.
- The committee reports selections to the Board [quarterly]; the Board reviews the program annually.

## 6. Donor Preferences

Consistent with the Donor Agreement (Section 3.3), a donor's selection of a student on the platform is a **non-binding preference**. The committee gives effect to preferences only where the preferred student is independently verified, eligible under this policy, and selected under Section 4 criteria. Funds preferred toward a fully funded or ineligible student are allocated to the next eligible student by rubric score. Donations conditioned on payment to a specific individual are declined.

## 7. Grant Amounts and Payment

- Grant amounts are set by documented educational cost (fee schedules, book lists), capped at [₹X / $Y] per student per academic year.
- **Payment is made directly to the educational institution wherever possible** (tuition, exam fees, hostel). Payments to the student or guardian are permitted only for expenses institutions cannot invoice (books, supplies, transport), against receipts or a spending plan, and are capped at [₹X] per disbursement.
- Multi-installment disbursement tied to continued enrollment is the default for grants above [₹X].
- For grants paid into India, disbursements comply with FCRA and are routed through [FCRA-registered partner organization / direct institutional payment — decide with counsel].

## 8. Supervision of Grants and Misuse Procedures

- Recipients (or guardians) sign a grant agreement stating the funds' educational purpose, reporting duties, and repayment obligation on misuse.
- Continued installments require: proof of continued enrollment each term, and academic progress reports [each term / annually].
- If the Foundation learns funds were diverted from educational use: further installments are suspended, the recipient is given [30] days to account, and unaccounted funds are demanded back. The committee documents the investigation and outcome; willful misuse permanently disqualifies the recipient.
- The Foundation withholds future grants from any institution that misapplies directly paid funds.

## 9. Records

For each grant, the Foundation retains for at least [7] years:
- The application, verification file, and committee scoring record
- Basis for selection (and for denials, the basis for denial)
- Grant agreement, disbursement records, and receipts
- Enrollment confirmations and progress reports
- Any misuse investigation records

Aggregate program data (number of applicants, selections, amounts, outcomes) is reported in the Foundation's annual report and Form 990.

## 10. Non-Discrimination

The Foundation selects recipients on an objective, nondiscriminatory basis. The applicant pool is broad and open — any student meeting Section 2 criteria may apply via the public platform — and selections are made without regard to race, religion, caste, gender, ethnicity, or relationship to any donor or insider.

## 11. Renewal

Grants are for one academic year. Recipients may reapply; renewal applications are re-verified for continued need and enrollment and scored alongside new applications. Renewal is not automatic.
